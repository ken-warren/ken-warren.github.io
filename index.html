About me

I'm Kennedy Kyalo — a statistics graduate (JKUAT) and data analyst who enjoys the moment a messy spreadsheet finally starts telling a clear story. I work across the whole chain: designing the questionnaire, collecting the data, cleaning it, modelling it and presenting what it means.

My toolkit is R, Python, STATA, SPSS, SQL and Power BI, grounded in statistical theory rather than guesswork. Whether it's a time-series forecast, an incentive model or an executive dashboard, the goal is the same: insight you can act on and defend.

Today I bring that to international trade at Tropical Heat Group, running export and import operations and the reporting and dashboards that guide them.
